## ⚖️ Section 2: The Non-Negotiable Book of Truths

### 🎯 A. Strategy & Statistical Discipline
* 🔹 **Truth 1: Strategy-Less Trading is Pure Gambling.** If a setup cannot be summarized in 2 non-discretionary rules (exact entry trigger + exact invalidation price), do NOT execute the trade.
* 🔹 **Truth 2: The 100-Trade Sample Law.** Execute ONE single strategy for 100 consecutive trades without changing indicators, timeframes, or position sizing before evaluating performance.

### 🛡️ B. Risk & Capital Protection
* 🔸 **Truth 3: Daily Loss Cap Overrides Everything.** Hard stop at **-₹1,000 (2% of capital)**. Once hit, close the trading terminal immediately.
* 🔸 **Truth 4: Maximum 4 Trades Per Day.** Low frequency is your only proven profitable state. Never exceed 4 trades in a single session.
* 🔸 **Truth 5: One Active Position Only.** Single-position sniper only. Never hold multiple stock/index options simultaneously (e.g., Nifty, BDL, Adani, CDSL).
* 🔸 **Truth 6: Max Premium Allocation.** Never risk or allocate more than **₹15,000–₹20,000** total across open positions.
* 🔸 **Truth 7: High Order Count is Financial Self-Harm.** On days with 25+ trades, friction and fees destroy profitability regardless of market calls.

### ⏳ C. Timing, Mindset & Execution
* 🔺 **Truth 8: The 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM Terminal Lock.** ZERO new trades between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Terminal must be minimized or locked during this chop window.
* 🔺 **Truth 9: Two Strikes and Out.** Two consecutive stop-outs on the same asset results in a mandatory trading ban on that ticker for the day.
* 🔺 **Truth 10: Decouple P&L From Strategy.** Never take a trade to "make back" money. Every entry must stand on technical setup merits alone.
* 🔺 **Truth 11: Stop-Loss is an Operational Cost.** An SL is a standard business expense, not a failure requiring immediate retaliatory action.
* 🔺 **Truth 12: Small Red Days are Victories.** A -₹1,000 day preserves your capital to trade another day; a -₹7,000 day ruins weeks of effort.
* 🔺 **Truth 13: The Physical Circuit Breaker.** Upon hitting the loss limit or when 11:00 AM arrives: activate Dhan's Kill Switch, shut down the screen, and walk outside for 20 minutes.
